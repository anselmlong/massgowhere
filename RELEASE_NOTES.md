# MassGoWhere: tester release, 2 October 2026

Thank you for helping test MassGoWhere! It finds a Catholic Mass in Singapore that you can still make from where you are, and tells you when to leave.

- **Website:** https://massgowhere.com (best on your phone)
- **Telegram:** [@massgowherebot](https://t.me/massgowherebot)

## What's new in this round

**Mass times are more up to date.**
- Times now come straight from myCatholicSG's live database. A few parishes had changed their times, and these are now correct (for example, St Michael's Saturday morning Mass is now 7:30am).
- Every church's bulletin for this week is read daily. Church pages show any one-off changes from it and link to the bulletin.

**A map under your answer.**
- After the Mass we suggest, scroll down to see a map of the churches around you. Each pin shows the next Mass you can make there:
  - Dark pin: the Mass we picked for you.
  - Lighter pins: the other churches listed below the map.
  - Grey pins: other churches nearby. Tap one to see why we didn't list it (usually its Mass is later, or the trip is longer).
- Use two fingers to move the map.

**A photo of the church** sits next to the time, so you know what you're looking for when you arrive. Tap it to see it bigger. Every church now has one.

**Sunday or Sunset Mass.**
- On the last step of the home page, choose **Sunday or Sunset Mass** to see only Masses that count for Sunday (Sunday, or Saturday from 4pm).
- Saturday evening Masses are marked **Sunset** everywhere.
- On Telegram, tap **Sunday or Sunset Mass** under any answer.

**Simpler home page.** Each step is one tap. On "Which Mass?", tapping a choice finds your Mass straight away.

**Clearer leave times.** "Leave by 7:09am" is the latest you can leave and still arrive 5 minutes early to prepare for Mass. If you planned a later departure, we also tell you when you'd get there.

**Church pages are easier to read.**
- Each day's Masses are on one line.
- Extras like parish events, getting there, sacraments and contacts are folded away until you tap them.

## Please try

1. **Find a Mass now** from where you are. Is the answer one you'd actually go to? Does "Leave by" feel right for how long the trip takes you?
2. **Scroll to the map.** Does it help you check the answer? Is anything on it confusing?
3. **Plan ahead:** tap "Change" next to "Leaving now" and pick tomorrow morning, or choose **Sunday or Sunset Mass**.
4. **Open your own parish's page** (tap the church name, or "Browse all churches"). Are the times, Adoration, Confession and bulletin right?
5. **Catch a Mass on the way:** from the home page, try a trip you often make.
6. **The Telegram bot:** share your location or send your postal code.

## Things we know about

- **Public holidays, Christmas and Holy Week:** times often change on these days, and we show a reminder to check with the parish rather than the changed times.
- **Very early mornings by bus & MRT:** trips are planned for when you'd actually leave. Before about 6:30am buses and trains run less often, so an early Mass at a nearby church can look further away than it feels.
- **Adoration and Confession** times are still unclear for about 12 parishes. We leave them out or mark them unconfirmed rather than guess.
- **Bulletin changes are read automatically** and may occasionally miss or misread something. Please tell us if you spot one.
- **The Telegram bot's Sunday or Sunset button** appears once the bot has been updated. If you don't see it yet, the website has it.

## Sending feedback

- **On the website:** scroll to the bottom and tap **Send feedback**.
- **On Telegram:** send `/feedback` to @massgowherebot.
- **On a church page:** "Something wrong or out of date? **Tell us**".

Screenshots help a lot, especially of a wrong time or a confusing screen. Tell us where you searched from and roughly when.
